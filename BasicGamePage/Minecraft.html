Description

Minecraft is a 3D blocky sandbox where the limit is your creativity. You can play in survival and brave the night or creative and build to your hearts content! Play by yourself or play with others! The possibilities are endless.

Game Details

  • Genre: Action, Sandbox, Open World
  • Session Type: Singleplayer, Multiplayer 2+
  • Time Commitment: Forever/Endless (No pressure to keep going)

Review
